## 4. Investment policy

**Return objective.** Keep the value of near-term money and earn interest on it. Growth is not a goal until the cash needs for tuition and the laptop are met and she is employed.

**Liquidity.** High. About $3,100 is needed within 6 months.

**Target allocation (current)**

| Bucket | Purpose | Target | Range | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash and high-interest savings | Tuition, laptop, emergency fund | 100% of savings | 90% to 100% | Chequing + HISA |
| TFSA | Longer-term reserve | HISA or 1-year cashable GIC | Cash only for now | TFSA |

**Target allocation (after graduation, to review)**

| Bucket | Target | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency fund (HISA) | 6 months of expenses | Fixed amount first |
| FHSA / TFSA (home goal, 4 to 5 years) | 40% equity / 60% fixed income | ±10% |
| RRSP (retirement, 35+ years) | 80% equity / 20% fixed income | ±10% |

**Permitted investments.** HISA, cashable GICs, and (after graduation) low-cost broad index ETFs or balanced funds.
**Not permitted.** Individual stocks, crypto, leverage, buy-now-pay-later, borrowing to invest.

**Rebalancing.** Not needed while she holds only cash. Once investing, rebalance yearly or when a bucket moves outside its range.

## 5. Credit and borrowing preferences
- Uses credit only to build history and as an emergency cushion. Pays the card in full and keeps usage under 30%.
- Prefers a **$2,000 line of credit with no co-signer**, used only for tuition. A parent co-signer is a backup only.
- No car loan before graduation.
- Mortgage: not before about 2032 and a stable full-time income.
