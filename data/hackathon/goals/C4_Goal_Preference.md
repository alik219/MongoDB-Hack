## 4. Treasury investment policy (surplus cash)

**Objectives, in order:** (1) preserve principal; (2) keep liquidity for operations and the seasonal peak; (3) earn a market return within these limits.

**Liquidity tiers**

| Tier | Purpose | Target | Permitted instruments | Max term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operating | Daily payments, payroll, suppliers | $250M minimum | Bank deposits, overnight sweep | Overnight |
| Reserve | Seasonal build, contingency | $200M | Government T-bills, bankers' acceptances / bank deposit notes, AAA money-market funds | 90 days |
| Strategic | Known future outflows (DC down payment, note maturities) | Matched to the outflow | Government and provincial bonds, high-grade bank paper, term deposits | 12 months, matched to the outflow date |

**Credit quality and concentration limits**

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Minimum rating | Short-term R-1 (middle) or equivalent; long-term A or better |
| Any single bank | 25% of the investment portfolio at most |
| Any single non-government issuer | 10% at most |
| Government of Canada and provinces | No limit |
| Weighted average maturity | 60 days or less |

**Excluded.** Equities, corporate high-yield, derivatives other than approved hedges, crypto-assets, structured notes, and any investment made for speculation.

**Hedging policy.** Hedges only to offset real exposures (USD purchases, floating-rate debt). FX: 50% hedged on a rolling 6 months (range 30% to 70%). Interest rates: at least 75% of debt fixed or swapped to fixed.

## 6. Governance and operating preferences
- **Approvals:** board approves new facilities and the treasury policy each year. VP Treasury and Controller both sign as officers. Dual approval on all payments over $1M.
- **Payment controls:** call-back verification for any supplier bank-detail change (added after the 2023 attempt), positive pay, and segregation of duties.
- **Reporting to the bank:** audited statements within 120 days of year end; quarterly compliance certificates; weekly cash forecasts from Jul to Dec; daily cash reports in Nov and Dec.
- **Data handling:** advisors see summary metrics and covenant results only. Account numbers are hidden. Every access is logged, with alerts on bulk export.
- **Communication:** quarterly treasury reviews (video), annual review in person, and credit reviews with a credit officer present.
- **ESG preference:** funding energy-efficiency projects at stores and DCs is a priority; green-loan pricing is welcome where offered.

## 7. Constraints and special circumstances
- Retail seasonality: about 40% of annual cash flow arrives in Q4.
- Inventory is about 69% of current assets, so the quick ratio stays low.
- DC mortgage approval depends on the appraisal and environmental report.
- After the DC down payment, cash falls to about $495M, close to the $450M floor.

## 8. Monitoring and review triggers
Review this policy if: Debt/EBITDA goes above 2.0x; interest cover falls below 8.0x; revolver usage stays above 60% for more than 30 days outside Nov to Dec; cash falls below $450M; there is a rating change; an acquisition, major capex or new facility goes over $100M; or a payment-fraud event or control failure occurs.
