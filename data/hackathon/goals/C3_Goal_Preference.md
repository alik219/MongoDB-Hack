**Return objective.** About 4% to 5% a year over inflation over a full market cycle. Because her income and net worth already depend on a tech company, the personal portfolio is used to diversify away from it.

**Permitted investments.** Broad ETFs, investment-grade bonds, GICs, balanced funds.
**Limits.** No single stock above 5%. **No extra overweight in software or tech.** No leverage to invest. No angel investments in other start-ups from personal accounts (she can revisit this after the business is sold).

**Corporate investments (Lumenwave).** Surplus cash beyond the 3-month reserve goes into GICs and short-term bond funds with no equity risk. A corporate investment policy will be set once the reserve reaches $1.0M.

**Rebalancing.** Twice a year, or when a class is outside its range.
